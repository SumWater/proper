\section{Conclusion}

Failure-memory retrieval should be treated as a proposal whose applicability depends on observable execution evidence. In the evaluated setting, conservative selection improves recovery relative to TF-IDF, Dense retrieval, and a fixed LLM Judge for both models. The broader lesson is conditional: history helps Qwen, hurts Mistral overall, and produces zero or weak effects in some failure strata. A credible failure-memory evaluation must therefore include No Memory, negative transfer, component ablations, zero cells, and concentration diagnostics alongside average gains.
